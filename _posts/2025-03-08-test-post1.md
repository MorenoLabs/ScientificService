## Introduction

Setting up a Jekyll blog with the Hyde theme has been an interesting journey. In this post, I'll share some of my thoughts about the process and why I chose this approach for my blog.

Scientific Operations is my platform for sharing ideas related to operational efficiency and systematic approaches to complex problems. I believe that applying scientific thinking to operational challenges can yield remarkable results.

## Why Jekyll?

Jekyll offers several advantages for a technical blog:

- **Markdown-based** - Writing in Markdown is intuitive and efficient
- **Version control** - Since everything is stored as text files, I can use Git for versioning
- **Customizable** - The theme can be adjusted to match my preferences
- **Fast** - Static sites load quickly and are easy to host
